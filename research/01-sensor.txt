WHOOP 5.0 / MG sensor, for a dog collar

WHAT IT IS
  Screenless fitness pod. Released May 2025.
  Size: 34.7 x 24 x 10.6 mm. Weight about 26.5 g.
  Water: IP68, submersible to 10 m. Rain and a puddle, not a swim session.
  Battery: about 14 days. Charge off the collar on the USB-C puck
  (about 120 minutes on the basic charger; wireless PowerPack is optional
  and charges on-wrist).
  Radio: Bluetooth Low Energy only. No Wi-Fi on the strap.
  Processor reported in a teardown: Ambiq ARM Cortex-M4 with Bluetooth.

WHAT IT SENSES
  Motion: accelerometer and gyroscope.
  The 6-axis part documented for firmware 50.42.1.0 is a TDK InvenSense
  ICM-45686. That chip has an on-chip pedometer (TDK APEX) that can report
  a step count, a cadence, and an activity class of Walk, Run, or Unknown.
  The pedometer runs on the accelerometer, not the gyro.
  Chip ranges, not WHOOP's configured ranges: accel up to +/-32 g,
  gyro up to +/-4000 dps. WHOOP does not publish which range it selected.
  Also on the pod, and not needed for this project: optical heart rate,
  SpO2, skin temperature (ams OSRAM AS6221), vibration motor (TI DRV2625).
  Optical front end documented as Analog Devices MAX86176.
  No barometer. No compass. No microphone. No speaker. No display.

5.0 VERSUS MG
  Same motion sensor. MG adds an ECG clasp and a revised sensor board.
  Serial prefix 5AG is a 5.0. Serial prefix 5AM is an MG.
  Hardware revision containing WG50 is a 5.0.
  Either pod works for steps and movement. Ignore ECG.

WHAT IT DOES NOT HAVE
  No GPS chip. "Connected GPS" in spec sheets means the phone's GPS while
  a workout is started in the official app. Reviews and WHOOP's own community
  staff say the band cannot record a route by itself.
  Ordinary step distance in the official app is a stride estimate, not GPS.
  WHOOP says steps come from the accelerometer, with a model that tries to
  ignore driving and cycling, plus a stride-length estimate. Wrist wear is
  what they validate. A dog neck is not that.
  BLE range is a room, not a yard and not a park. This cannot answer
  "where is the dog."

HOW IT TALKS
  BLE peripheral. Custom service:
    fd4b0001-cce1-4033-93ce-002d5875f58a
  Command write: fd4b0002 (same suffix).
  Notify: fd4b0003, fd4b0004, fd4b0005, fd4b0007.
  Frames are WHOOP 5 format: 0xAA, format 0x01, length, CRC16-Modbus over
  the first 6 bytes, inner record at byte 8, CRC32 at the tail.
  A second phone or the official app left bonded will fight the link.
  Pair one central.

ON-DEVICE MEMORY
  The useful motion log is the historical store, not a live stream from
  the yard. The offline client treats a full offload as roughly the last
  14 days. Pull it when the collar is in the room. Do not depend on a
  live BLE session outdoors.
