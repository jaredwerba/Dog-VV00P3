Weekend plan: daily dog movement from an old WHOOP 5.0 or MG

Goal: steps, movement class, and a rough distance. No heart rate.
Not a map. Not a live location.

SATURDAY MORNING — prove the pipe on a person first
  1. Pair the pod with the offline client only. Forget it from the
     official WHOOP app first, or the two centrals fight.
     Confirm the serial. 5AG is 5.0. 5AM is MG. Either is fine.
  2. Wear it on a wrist. Walk a counted 100 steps, or one measured block.
     Sync.
  3. Export the raw CSV. Pass only if all three are true:
       the counter at byte 57 rose by about 100
       activity class flipped from 0 (still) to 1 (walk)
       gravity magnitude sat near 1 g while the pod was still
     If the counter did not move, stop. Do not mount it on the dog
     until that field is live. The fallback is the 1 Hz dynamic-g
     peak detector the app already has. Worse pedometer, still a log.

SATURDAY AFTERNOON — collar and a stride constant
  4. Collar, not a wrist strap. The pod is 10.6 mm thick and 26.5 g.
     On a loose collar it sits under the jaw or spins. Sew or tape it
     into a sleeve so the long axis stays fixed relative to the neck.
     Snug matters for the pedometer. IP68 covers rain, not a bath.
  5. Calibrate on this dog. Walk a measured 100 m, or one loop whose
     length you already know, collar on, then sync.
     stride_m = meters / counter_delta
     Do it twice: a walk and a trot. Class 1 and class 2 will not share
     a stride. Expect 20 to 40 percent error. That is enough to tell a
     lazy day from zoomies. It is not a training log.
     The 0.83 m preset in Motion.swift is a starting guess for a dog
     over 25 kg. Replace it with the measured number.
  6. Leave the optical LEDs alone. Do not spend the weekend trying to
     disable heart rate.

SUNDAY — the daily loop
  7. Charge on the USB-C puck every 10 to 12 days, off the collar.
  8. Once a day, bring the collar into the room and pull history.
     Do not stream from the yard. The data is already on the pod.
  9. Reduce. Do not build a dashboard first.
       steps     = last counter minus first, inside the time window,
                   plus 65536 if the delta went negative
       active    = seconds with class 1 or 2
       rest      = seconds with class 0
       distance  = steps times the measured stride
       thrash    = dynamic g above about 0.3 g, sustained
                   (rolling and wrestling that a pedometer under-counts)
  10. One page: date, steps, active minutes, estimated km, and a
      still/walk/run timeline. SQLite is already the history store
      in this project. A chart is the last hour, not the first.

TRAPS
  The counter is cumulative. Always difference it inside a window.
  A neck is not a wrist. Head bobs add steps. Short bursts get missed.
  First week is calibration, not truth.
  Two bonds at once will look like a dead strap.
